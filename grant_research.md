## SECTION 1: RECOVERY HOUSING / SOBER LIVING GRANTS

### 1A. SAMHSA — Building Communities of Recovery (BCOR)
- **Funder:** Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (federal)
- **Amount:** Up to $300,000/year
- **Duration:** Typically 4–5 years
- **Match Required:** Yes — $15 non-federal for every $85 federal (roughly 15% match)
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofit recovery community organizations governed principally by people in recovery. 501(c)(3) required.
- **Deadline:** FY2026 NOFO not yet released as of May 2026. Watch samhsa.gov/grants/grants-dashboard/forecasts
- **Apply:** grants.gov + samhsa.gov/grants
- **Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** HIGH. FARR-certified sober living homes run by people in recovery are a direct match. This is the flagship federal grant for exactly this type of organization.
- **Note:** Florida has previously received BCOR funding. New states/territories that haven't received BCOR funding get a 10-point bonus on scoring — Florida may not qualify for that bonus, but the program is still open.

---

### 1B. SAMHSA — Recovery Community Services Program (RCSP)
- **Funder:** SAMHSA (federal)
- **Amount:** Up to $300,000/year
- **Eligibility:** Peer-run nonprofit organizations providing recovery support services. 501(c)(3) required.
- **Deadline:** Watch samhsa.gov/grants for FY2026 NOFO
- **Apply:** grants.gov
- **Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** HIGH. If the sober living home involves peer-led recovery support services (which FARR-certified homes typically do), this is a strong match.
- **Note:** This is distinct from BCOR — RCSP is broader and supports peer support networks, not just community organizing.

---

### 1C. Florida Opioid Settlement Abatement Funds
- **Funder:** State of Florida / Statewide Council on Opioid Abatement (via settlements with opioid manufacturers)
- **Amount:** $17.8 million allocated statewide for recovery housing in FY2024–2025; similar allocations expected annually
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofits providing evidence-based, NARR/FARR-certified recovery housing. 501(c)(3) preferred. Counties also distribute their own shares.
- **Deadline:** Rolling RFPs issued by the Statewide Council and by individual county health departments
- **Apply:** 
  - State level: floridaopioidsettlement.com — contact the Statewide Council on Opioid Abatement
  - Broward County level: Contact Broward County Human Services + Broward Behavioral Health Coalition (bbhcflorida.org)
  - Miami-Dade level: Contact Miami-Dade County Health Department
- **Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** HIGH. Florida is actively spending $17.8M/year on recovery housing. FARR certification is essentially required to be competitive. First priority: get on the county health department's radar before the certification is complete.
- **Note:** Broward County specifically contracts with FARR-certified homes to house individuals exiting treatment, jail, or homelessness. This is also a revenue stream (per diem bed contracts), not just a grant.

---

### 1D. HUD Continuum of Care (CoC) — Transitional Housing
- **Funder:** U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development
- **Amount:** $100,000–$400,000 per award
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofits or government agencies serving individuals experiencing homelessness. Recovery residences serving homeless individuals qualify. Must apply through local CoC collaborative.
- **Deadline:** FY2024–25 NOFO closed Feb 9, 2026. Next cycle expected to open January 2027.
- **Apply:** Through the local Continuum of Care:
  - Broward County CoC: Broward Partnership for the Homeless (browardpartner.org)
  - Miami-Dade CoC: Miami-Dade Homeless Trust (homelesstrust.org)
- **Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** HIGH (medium-term). Must be established and serving homeless individuals in recovery. Get registered with the local CoC now so you're in position for the January 2027 NOFO.
- **Note:** The Broward County CoC is actively funded. CoC grants fund both housing operations AND wraparound services.

---

### 1E. HUD Recovery Housing Program (RHP) — via FloridaJobs.org
- **Funder:** HUD, administered by Florida Department of Commerce
- **Amount:** Varies (competitive awards for transitional housing infrastructure)
- **Eligibility:** Authorized under Section 8071 of the SUPPORT Act. Provides stable, transitional housing for individuals in recovery from SUD. State administers funds.
- **Deadline:** State issues RFPs as funds become available
- **Apply:** floridajobs.org/RHP — Contact Florida Department of Commerce, Community Development Division
- **Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** HIGH. This is purpose-built for exactly what Jimmy Safe Place does. FARR certification will be required.
- **Note:** This is separate from CoC. Specifically targets substance use disorder recovery housing — not just general homelessness.

## SECTION 2: ANIMAL RESCUE / REHABILITATION GRANTS (PAWsitive Recovery, Inc.)

### 2A. Petco Love (formerly Petco Foundation) — Animal Welfare Grants
- **Funder:** Petco Love (national nonprofit)
- **Amount:** $5,000–$750,000 (most awards $5K–$50K; larger awards exist)
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits whose PRIMARY mission is companion animal welfare. Organizations must be registered as a Petco Love partner.
- **Deadlines:** 
  - Shelter/adoption grant cycle: February 1 – March 31 (closed for 2026; next cycle Feb 2027)
  - Spay/neuter + TNR cycle: September 1–30, 2026 (OPEN in ~3.5 months)
- **Apply:** petcolove.org/shelter-partners/grants — Must first become a Petco Love partner organization
- **Fit for PAWsitive Recovery:** HIGH. The September cycle for spay/neuter and medical care programs is a natural fit. The dual recovery model (rescued animals + sober residents) is compelling storytelling for Petco Love's mission.
- **Note:** Annual partnership report with animal intake/outcome data required. Start tracking this data now.

---

### 2B. PetSmart Charities — Partner Grants
- **Funder:** PetSmart Charities (national)
- **Amount:** Up to $250,000 (performance-based for adoption partners); smaller grants for emerging organizations
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits enriching lives through the human-animal bond. Must become a PetSmart Charities Adoption Partner to access larger grants.
- **Deadline:** Rolling; annual grant cycle
- **Apply:** petsmartcharities.org/pro/grants
- **Fit for PAWsitive Recovery:** HIGH. PetSmart Charities specifically funds organizations "enriching lives through the human-animal bond" — PAWsitive Recovery's model of pairing rescue animals with people in recovery is a textbook match for this language. Becoming an in-store adoption partner also opens the door to the $250K performance grant.
- **Note:** PetSmart distributes $46M+ per year. Getting in their partner network is the first step.

### 4A. SAMHSA State Opioid Response (SOR) — via Florida DCF
- **Funder:** SAMHSA → Florida DCF → County/managing entity
- **Amount:** Florida receives $1.575 billion+ nationally; state distributes via subgrants. Individual awards vary.
- **Eligibility:** Organizations providing evidence-based treatment and recovery services for opioid/stimulant use disorder. FARR certification expected.

## SECTION 6: NOTES FOR GRANT WRITERS

### Jimmy Safe Place — Positioning
- FARR certification is non-negotiable for government grants. Do not apply to state/federal recovery housing grants without it.
- Lead with: FARR-certified recovery residence + community benefit (reduced recidivism, housing stability for individuals in recovery)
- For Broward County funders, emphasize that the first home is in Broward and will serve Broward residents
- The address (Miami FL 33055) is technically Miami-Dade. Clarify service area in applications: is the *home* in Broward or Miami-Dade? This matters for county-level grants.

### PAWsitive Recovery — Positioning
- The "dual healing" model (rescued animals + sober residents) is genuinely unique and fundable from BOTH animal welfare AND human services angles
- For animal welfare funders (Petco Love, PetSmart, ASPCA): lead with the rescue/rehabilitation mission; the human recovery element is the differentiator
- For human services funders (SAMHSA, Florida DCF, Florida Blue): lead with the recovery housing mission; the animal element is the evidence-based therapeutic modality (animal-assisted therapy has strong research backing via HABRI)
- SPCA International already runs a "PAWsitive Recovery" program (spcai.org) — James should be aware of this and may want to coordinate or differentiate the name
- Build outcome tracking from day one: animal intake/outcomes AND resident recovery metrics. Both are required by animal welfare funders and are compelling evidence for human services funders.

### Cost Flagging
- FARR certification fee: Initial application fee not publicly disclosed; renewal capped at $100/yr. Contact farronline.org for current application fee.
- Candid Gold Seal: FREE via candid.org
- PetSmart Charities partner application: FREE
- Petco Love partner registration: FREE
- HumanePro grant listings: FREE at humanepro.org/grant-listings
- Instrumentl grant database: PAID subscription (~$200/mo) — not necessary if using free resources above
- GrantStation: PAID subscription — only needed for AAT Fund; check if a local library offers free access first
