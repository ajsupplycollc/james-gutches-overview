### 1. Banfield Foundation — Community Care Grant
**Target Org:** PAWsitive Recovery
**Status:** OPEN — Applications accepted quarterly
**Next Deadline:** July 1, 2026 (then October 1, 2026)
**Amount:** $5,000–$15,000
**What it funds:** Veterinary care delivered to owned pets through on-site, mobile, or pop-up clinics. Covers medications, vaccines, and medical supplies — NOT operating overhead or salaries.

### 15. Second Chance Act — Community-Based Reentry Program (BJA/DOJ)
**Target Org:** Jimmy Safe Place
**Status:** FY25 NOFO recently closed (deadline passed April/May 2026). FY26 NOFO not yet released as of May 2026. Typically released Jan–Mar annually.
**Amount:** Up to $1,000,000 per award (36-month period); approximately 13 awards projected nationally
**What it funds:** Mentoring and transitional services for adults returning from incarceration who are assessed as moderate-to-high risk for recidivism. Covers transitional housing, mentoring, job training, case management.
**Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits and federally recognized tribal governments. Must serve adults post-incarceration.
**Fit for Jimmy Safe Place:** Strong conceptually — sober living houses serving formerly incarcerated individuals align with this program. However, this requires active criminal justice engagement, not just recovery housing generally. If their resident population includes justice-involved individuals, this is a top-tier federal opportunity.
**Action:** Monitor https://bja.ojp.gov/funding/current for FY26 NOFO announcement. Expected early 2027.
**Verified:** Yes — grants.gov NOFO listing confirmed; FY25 deadline confirmed closed
